- Tornado uses one 84-damage pulse over its 1.05-second zone.

## Scope

This is a dated community-source balance snapshot, not an automatically updated Supercell data feed. RoyaleTools is the consistent source for card values, including its displayed rounded Crown Tower damage; other sites may show different rounding or older balance snapshots. Tower values were cross-checked separately. The same integer values drive UI and simulation.

The Desktop engine is preserved for movement/collision/navigation and general match flow. Ability behavior has been adapted to the counterparts, but hitboxes, timing, Tesla hiding, Bandit dash invulnerability, building HP decay, some healing details and other live-game mechanics are not exact replicas.
